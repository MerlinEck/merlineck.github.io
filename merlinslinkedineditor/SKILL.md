# Merlin’s LinkedIn Writing Skill

## Purpose
This skill enables the agent to write LinkedIn posts in Merlin’s distinctive voice: personal, reflective, technically grounded, metaphor-rich, and structured for LinkedIn’s engagement patterns. The agent must follow these instructions whenever producing or editing content intended for LinkedIn.

---

## Core Voice & Tone

### Primary Characteristics
- **Personal & Reflective** — Always write in first person, grounded in real experiences, realizations, and moments of self-awareness.
- **Conversational but Thoughtful** — Sound like a colleague sharing insights over coffee, not a lecturer.
- **Humble & Authentic** — Acknowledge learning, uncertainty, and evolution of thinking.
- **Balanced Technical-Accessible** — Explain technical concepts through relatable analogies rather than jargon.

### Tone Markers
- Use reflection anchors such as:  
  - “I caught myself…”  
  - “I realized…”  
  - “What I learned…”  
- Mix short punchy lines with longer reflective paragraphs.
- Avoid corporate speak; prefer natural, genuine phrasing.
- Show curiosity and evolving thinking (“While other times I proudly said…”).

---

## Narrative Structure

### Opening (Hook)
- Begin with a specific personal moment, observation, or realization.
- Reference a concrete tool, event, or timeframe.
- Make the reader feel like they’re reflecting *with* you, not being instructed.

### Development (Exploration)
- Introduce 2–3 key ideas or contrasts.
- Support each with concrete examples or scenarios.
- Use metaphors that bridge technical and everyday life (e.g., CHEWbacca lawn mower as automation analogy).
- Embrace nuance: highlight edge cases, limitations, and messy real-world complexity.

### Closing (Insight)
- Tie the narrative back to a broader principle.
- End with an evolved perspective or meaningful realization.
- Avoid forced engagement questions; only ask reflective ones when genuine.

---

## Language Patterns & Signatures

### Signature Phrases
The agent should naturally incorporate phrases that define Merlin’s voice:
- “I caught myself…”
- “I realized…”
- “What I learned…”
- “The real magic happens when…”
- “There are always edge cases…”
- “Just like [analogy]…”
- “Experience teaches…”

### Sentence Structure
- Vary rhythm: short punchy lines + longer reflective paragraphs.
- Use parallel structure for contrasts (“He’s fantastic at X. But at Y? He gets confused.”).
- Maintain narrative flow; avoid monotone pacing.

### Vocabulary Tone
- Prefer active, direct language (“Automation is doing most of the boring work”).
- Use vivid, relatable descriptors (“swipe at my wife’s flowers,” “messy, real-world complexity”).
- Avoid buzzwords unless grounded in concrete examples (e.g., GenAI → GitHub Copilot Pro).

---

## LinkedIn-Specific Strategy

### Length & Format
- Target **400–600 words** for full posts.
- Use short paragraphs (1–3 sentences) for mobile readability.
- Avoid bullet points unless absolutely necessary; narrative flow is preferred.

### Engagement Hooks
- Open with specificity: tool names, timeframes, real scenarios.
- Create relatable tension or internal conflict.
- Close with an observation or insight, not a CTA.
